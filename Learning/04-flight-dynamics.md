# How to study

Draw a free-body diagram of the quadrotor. Identify the thrust produced by each motor and how changing motor thrust creates roll, pitch or yaw. Use simple numerical examples before applying the model to the mixer.

# Labs

1) Draw the force diagram. 2) Mark motor rotation directions. 3) Calculate simple thrust-to-weight examples. 4) Map roll/pitch/yaw commands to motor changes. 5) Verify mixer logic without propellers.

# Gandaberunda connection

Flight dynamics connects desired pilot commands and controller outputs to the physical four-motor aircraft.

# Must know

Thrust vs weight; torque; roll/pitch/yaw; centre of gravity; motor directions; motor mixing.

# Pass test

Both developers can explain how a quadrotor creates vertical thrust, roll, pitch and yaw and can explain the basic mixer behaviour.
