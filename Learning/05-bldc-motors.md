# How to study

Start by identifying the motor's physical parts. Learn how changing phase excitation creates a rotating magnetic field. Relate electrical power to torque/RPM and then understand how the propeller load changes motor behaviour.

# Labs

1) Identify motor wires/components. 2) Read the motor datasheet. 3) Compare KV ratings conceptually. 4) Observe safe no-prop motor operation. 5) Measure current only with an appropriate controlled setup. 6) Compare motor/propeller requirements.

# Gandaberunda connection

BLDC motors are the actuators that turn ESC commands into propeller thrust. Motor choice affects current, thrust, efficiency and flight performance.

# Must know

BLDC vs brushed; phases; commutation; KV; torque; RPM; current; efficiency; motor/propeller matching.

# Pass test

Both developers can explain how electrical commutation produces motor rotation and why motor/propeller selection affects current and thrust.
