# Explanation

## 1. Cell voltage

Voltage is the electrical potential difference that provides the push for charge to move. In Gandaberunda, every component has an allowed voltage range; a wrong rail can cause malfunction or permanent damage.

## 6. Energy

Energy describes how much work a battery can provide over time. A battery's usable energy helps determine expected endurance, while the propulsion load determines how quickly that energy is consumed.

## 7. Current capability

Current is the rate at which electric charge flows. Propulsion can draw large current, while the STM32 and sensors usually require much smaller currents. Wiring, connectors and regulators must be selected for the current they actually carry.

# How to study

Learn the electrical meaning of a LiPo pack before selecting one. Calculate nominal voltage, approximate energy and current capability. Understand that battery selection is a system decision based on motors, ESCs and electronics.

# Labs

1) Identify pack configuration. 2) Measure pack voltage safely. 3) Read the battery specification. 4) Calculate energy. 5) Estimate current requirement from the propulsion system. 6) Record safe handling procedures.

# Gandaberunda connection

The battery supplies propulsion and regulated electronics. Its voltage, capacity and current capability affect flight time, performance and safety.

# Must know

Cell count; nominal/max voltage; capacity; C-rating; energy; current capability; safe charging/storage; connectors.

# Pass test

Both developers can read a LiPo specification, calculate basic pack voltage/energy and explain why the selected pack must match the propulsion and power architecture.
