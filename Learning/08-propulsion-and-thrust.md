## 9. Current draw

Current is the rate at which electric charge flows. Propulsion can draw large current, while the STM32 and sensors usually require much smaller currents. Wiring, connectors and regulators must be selected for the current they actually carry.

# How to study

Understand propulsion as one system rather than choosing motors, ESCs and propellers independently. Use manufacturer data and controlled measurements where available. Learn why higher thrust can also mean higher current.

# Labs

1) Calculate required thrust-to-weight ratio for an example. 2) Compare propeller sizes conceptually. 3) Read a motor thrust table. 4) Build a propulsion current estimate. 5) Verify motor direction without propellers.

# Gandaberunda connection

Propulsion determines whether Gandaberunda can lift, hover and respond to control commands while staying within battery and ESC limits.

# Must know

Thrust-to-weight; propeller diameter/pitch; KV; RPM; current; efficiency; matching.

# Pass test

Both developers can justify a motor/propeller/ESC/battery combination from requirements and explain the trade-off between thrust, current and efficiency.
